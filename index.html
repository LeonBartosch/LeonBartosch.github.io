About

Fascinated by why music moves us.

As a composer, I am constantly exploring how music shapes emotion, reveals character and becomes part of a story.

That same curiosity also led me to pursue doctoral research into timbre and auditory perception. Both disciplines continue to inspire one another and shape the way I think about music.
